Keeping the Internet Safe and secure.

NetSSR is a non-profit research association contributing, at international scale, to the security, and safety of the Internet and the digital infrastructure it runs on.

§1 — Object

Four commitments, one purpose..

Article 2 of our statutes defines our purpose along four dimensions.

§2 — Activities

What we actually run.

  • RESEARCH

    Internet measurement & research

    Studying and documenting how the DNS, routing, protocols and critical infrastructure of the Internet function — and publishing what we find.

  • THREAT-INTEL

    Abuse detection & threat intelligence

    Detecting and documenting phishing, malware distribution, domain abuse, fraudulent infrastructure and emerging threats to trust online. You can visit URLAbuse project which launched in 2023 with the mission of collecting and sharing URL intelligence with the Cybersecurity community to make the Internet safer for everyone.

  • OPEN-SOURCE

    Tools, data & infrastructure

    Building and maintaining open software, platforms, observatories, block lists and datasets that improve the security and resilience of the Internet ecosystem.

  • EDUCATION

    Publications & training

    Conferences, workshops, training sessions and written work aimed at growing public understanding of digital security.

  • COOPERATION

    Cross-sector cooperation

    Connecting researchers, engineers, network operators, academia, civil society and industry around a shared, open Internet.
